Why LivTech

Build something meaningful

Join a team that's passionate about using technology to transform senior care. Here's what makes LivTech a great place to work.

Innovation First

Work with cutting-edge technologies including AI/ML, modern cloud infrastructure, and the latest development tools. We're building the next generation of healthcare software.

Mission-Driven Impact

Your work directly improves the lives of seniors and their caregivers. We're transforming long-term care by providing better tools for those who need them most.

Growth & Learning

Continuous learning is part of our DNA. We invest in your professional development through mentorship, training, conferences, and hands-on experience with new technologies.

Collaborative Culture

Work alongside talented engineers, designers, and healthcare experts. We believe the best solutions come from diverse perspectives working together.

Autonomy & Ownership

Take ownership of meaningful projects from day one. We trust our team members to make decisions and drive initiatives that shape our products.

Work-Life Balance

Flexible hybrid work, generous PTO, and a culture that respects your time outside of work. We believe happy, well-rested teams build better products.

Our Values

Startup culture with PE backing

We move fast, think big, and have the resources to make it happen. These are the values that guide how we work together.

Humility

We don't like working with jerks, no matter how smart they are. The best ideas can come from anywhere, and we stay open to learning from everyone.

Honesty

We communicate openly and directly. Trust is built through transparency, and we believe in giving and receiving candid feedback.

Extreme Ownership

We all make mistakes; owning up to them and learning from them is how we get better and earn respect. No finger-pointing, just solutions.

Work Hard and Have Fun

We bring energy and enthusiasm to challenging problems. Great work doesn't have to feel like a grind when you're surrounded by people you enjoy working with.

Passion for Quality

We take pride in our craft and hold ourselves to high standards. We build the best software we can and don't settle for less.

Be Smart and Gets Things Done

We value people who can think critically and execute effectively. Pair program with AI; you drive, it builds, allowing us to ship solutions quickly.

Attention to Detail

The little things matter. We sweat the details because we know that excellence is built on a foundation of small things done right.
